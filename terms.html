Program Description

JustGoodTimes Event Invitations & Updates sends text messages to notify users of event invitations and updates from event organizers using the JustGoodTimes platform.

How You're Enrolled

You may enroll by creating an account at just-good-times.com and entering your mobile number, or by being added to an event by an organizer, in which case you'll receive a one-time invitation text with instructions to opt in or opt out.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your event activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-Out

Reply STOP at any time to cancel. You will receive a one-time confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages will be sent unless you opt in again.

Help

Reply HELP for assistance, or contact ameyer@just-good-times.com.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

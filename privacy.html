Privacy Policy

JustGoodTimes LLC — Effective August 19, 2026

JustGoodTimes LLC ("JustGoodTimes," "we," "us") operates just-good-times.com and a related event invitation and update service. This policy explains how we handle your information, including your mobile phone number and SMS messaging data.

Information We Collect

We collect your mobile phone number when you create an account, or when an event organizer adds your number to invite you to an event. We may also collect your name and event-related activity.

SMS Consent and Non-Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or your SMS opt-in consent with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Your number is used solely to send you event invitations and updates through JustGoodTimes.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your event activity and the number of events you are invited to or organizing. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting Out

You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. You may also reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at ameyer@just-good-times.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to ameyer@just-good-times.com.
